Buyer’s guide

What is the best free browser-based job application tracker?

It depends on whether you want to find jobs, log them, or have AI write for you. Here are the criteria, and where each option lands.

HireWire · last reviewed 2026-10-15

Short answer

  • Best for finding and tracking with no account: HireWire. It ranks 55 live job feeds against your profile and tracks the jobs you save, all inside your browser. It has no manual entry.
  • Best for manual tracking with no account: JobsTracker (jobstracker.org). You add any application, it reads pasted recruiter emails, and it offers optional encryption.
  • Best cloud suite with AI writing: Huntr or Teal. Both need an account. Huntr’s free plan tracks up to 100 jobs. Teal’s free plan has unlimited tracking.
  • Most flexible: a spreadsheet, if you don’t mind doing all the work yourself.

Disclosure: this page is published by HireWire. We have tried to state where other tools are better, and we mark anything we couldn’t verify.

Criteria that actually matter

  1. Real cost. Is tracking free without a limit, or is there a cap that forces an upgrade mid-search?
  2. Account and data location. Does your search history sit on a vendor’s server, or only on your device?
  3. Getting jobs in. Do you type every role, clip it from a site, or does the tool find postings for you?
  4. Exit. Can you export everything in an open format (JSON or CSV) whenever you want?
  5. Follow-through. Does it tell you what has gone quiet and what to do next?
  6. Durability. Browser-only tools can lose data when site storage is cleared. Is there a one-step backup?

Where HireWire fits, honestly

HireWire is strongest if your search is mainly remote or tech roles. Its feeds are remote boards (Remotive, RemoteOK, We Work Remotely and others) and career pages of companies such as Stripe, OpenAI, Linear and Vercel, plus German employers such as N26, Celonis, Trade Republic and Delivery Hero. You can widen coverage with a free Adzuna key. It scores each posting by your skills, your salary floor in your own currency, freshness and blocked keywords. It also drafts follow-ups when an application goes quiet for 14 days.

It is weakest if most of your applications come from outside its feeds: LinkedIn, local job sites or recruiter emails. HireWire can only save postings from its feeds. Pair it with a spreadsheet, or choose a manual tracker.

A simple way to choose

  • You want something to surface roles each morning: HireWire.
  • You apply everywhere and only need a log: JobsTracker or a spreadsheet.
  • You want résumé rewriting and autofill and don’t mind an account: Huntr or Teal.
  • Your search is confidential: pick a no-account tool, then read what to check before trusting a free tracker.

FAQ

What is the best free browser-based job application tracker?

For finding and tracking jobs with no account, HireWire. It ranks 55 public job feeds against your profile and keeps everything in your browser. For logging applications by hand with no account, JobsTracker (jobstracker.org). For a cloud suite with AI résumé tools, Huntr or Teal.

Is there a job tracker that doesn’t need an account?

Yes. HireWire and JobsTracker (jobstracker.org) both run entirely in your browser with no sign-up. A local spreadsheet also needs none.

Are browser-based job trackers safe from data loss?

Only if you back up. Browser storage is deleted when you clear site data, and Safari can delete it after 7 days without a visit. Use the tool’s export regularly. HireWire shows a reminder when your backup is out of date.
