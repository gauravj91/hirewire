HireWire vs Huntr

Huntr is a polished cloud platform with AI résumé tools. HireWire is a free, no-account, browser-only alternative for people who would rather their job search not live on someone else’s server.

HireWire · last reviewed 2026-10-15

Verdict

  • Huntr is the better fit if you want an all-in-one cloud suite: a Chrome job clipper that saves jobs from any site, application autofill, AI résumé tailoring and cover letters, document storage, a mobile app and sync across devices. The free plan tracks up to 100 jobs. Pro is $40/month.
  • HireWire is the better fit if privacy and price come first. There is no account, nothing is stored on a server, and the core app is free; only the optional AI fit score is paid, as a one-time pass rather than a subscription. It also finds jobs for you from 55 public feeds and scores them. It has no clipper and no autofill. AI cover letters and tailored CV drafts come with the optional Pass Plus (€29 for 30 days, one-time), and encrypted sync with any pass.

Why someone looks for a Huntr alternative

The two common reasons are cost and data. Huntr Pro costs $40 a month. The free plan caps tracking at 100 jobs, which an active search can reach in a few weeks. And a cloud tracker holds a detailed record of your search: who you applied to, your notes, your résumé. That record sits under an account with a list of service providers. For many people that’s fine. For people searching while still employed, or who simply don’t want that record held anywhere, a local tool is the cleaner option.

What you give up by switching to HireWire

Be clear-eyed about the gaps. HireWire can’t clip a job from LinkedIn or a company site. It only tracks postings that appear on its feeds, which lean towards remote and tech roles. It has no résumé builder or templates; AI writing needs Pass Plus or your own key. If you rely on the clipper or autofill daily, Huntr will save you more time.

What you gain

You get a tracker with no sign-up, no cap and no subscription, where everything you type stays on your device. You also get job discovery built in: an hourly-refreshed, ranked list from 55 feeds, filtered by your salary floor and blocked locations, with a follow-up draft when an application goes quiet.

FAQ

Is HireWire a free alternative to Huntr?

Yes, for job tracking and discovery. HireWire is free with no account and no job limit. It doesn’t replace Huntr’s résumé builder, Chrome clipper or autofill.

Does Huntr require an account?

Yes. Huntr’s free and Pro plans both start with a sign-up, and your data is stored in Huntr’s cloud.

How much is Huntr Pro?

Per huntr.co/pricing (checked 2026-10-14): $40 billed monthly, $90 every 3 months, or $160 every 6 months.

Can I import my Huntr data into HireWire?

No. HireWire has no importer for other trackers. Keep your Huntr history in a spreadsheet and start fresh in HireWire for new roles.
