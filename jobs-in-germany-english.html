English-speaking jobs in Germany: one free job list

If you work in English and want a job in Germany, or remote from Germany, the postings are scattered across company career pages and a few boards. HireWire puts them in one list, ranks them against your skills and keeps your search in your browser.

HireWire · last reviewed 2026-10-22

Short answer

  • HireWire is a free job aggregator: 55 public job feeds in one list, refreshed hourly while the tab is open, with no account.
  • Germany is covered, not exclusive: career pages of German employers such as N26, Celonis, Trade Republic, Delivery Hero, DeepL, SIXT and Bosch, plus Arbeitnow, a board focused on jobs in Germany. Many other feeds are international and remote.
  • Honest limits: HireWire doesn’t detect the language of a posting. Use blocked keywords to push German-only roles down (below), and check each posting’s language requirements.

Which feeds have jobs in Germany

HireWire reads public job feeds straight from your browser. For Germany, the useful ones are:

  • Company career pages of employers headquartered in Germany: N26, Trade Republic, Raisin, Delivery Hero, HelloFresh, GetYourGuide, Contentful, Enpal, n8n and Thermondo (Berlin), Celonis, Helsing and SIXT (Munich area), DeepL (Cologne), Bosch (Stuttgart area), 1KOMMA5° (Hamburg) and Chrono24 (Karlsruhe). Many of these companies hire for roles where English is the working language; the posting says which.
  • International companies with offices in Germany whose global boards are in the feed list, so their German roles appear alongside the rest.
  • Arbeitnow, a job board focused on Germany and Europe.
  • Remote boards (Remotive, Jobicy, RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, Hacker News “Who is hiring?”), for remote roles open to people living in Germany or the EU.
  • Optional, with your own free key: Adzuna (country: Germany) adds a much larger set of German postings. Your key stays in your browser.

The full source list and how the feeds are read are in the guide.

How to find English-speaking roles in Germany with HireWire

  1. Set your role and place. Open HireWire, enter your skills (or build a CV and hand it over in one click), the city you want and whether you want remote, hybrid or on-site.
  2. Set a salary floor in EUR. Postings that list pay below it drop down the list; many German postings list no pay at all, which costs a few points rather than hiding them.
  3. Push German-only roles down. Add blocked keywords such as fließend Deutsch, Deutschkenntnisse, verhandlungssicher or German C1. Each match lowers a posting’s score sharply, so roles that require German sink to the bottom while still being visible.
  4. Hide what you can’t take. Blocked locations remove postings from places you won’t move to.
  5. Check the posting. Before applying, read the language requirements, and look at the ghost-job risk and the salary check.

What HireWire won’t do

  • It doesn’t show every job in Germany. StepStone, Indeed and LinkedIn are not feeds (see StepStone oder Indeed?, in German).
  • It doesn’t include the Bundesagentur für Arbeit job search.
  • It doesn’t translate postings or detect their language, and its interface is in English.

Applying in Germany: the CV question

For international and English-speaking teams, a one-page or two-page English CV without a photo is usually fine. More traditional German employers often expect a tabular Lebenslauf, sometimes with a photo, which is common but not required. The free CV builder does both: three ATS-friendly English templates and a German Lebenslauf (German version), PDF download, no sign-up, and your CV stays in your browser.

FAQ

Is there a job aggregator for English-speaking jobs in Germany?

HireWire is a free one: it combines 55 public job feeds, including career pages of German employers and Arbeitnow, a Germany-focused board, into one list ranked by your skills. It doesn’t filter by posting language, so use blocked keywords such as “fließend Deutsch” to push German-only roles down.

Do I need an account?

No. HireWire has no accounts. Your profile, saved jobs and notes stay in your browser’s storage on your device.

Is it free?

Yes. The feeds, ranking, tracker, ghost-job and salary checks and the CV builder are free. Only the optional AI features are paid, after 3 free fit scores and 1 free Reach out draft: a one-time pass from €9 covers both, no subscription.

Does HireWire include StepStone, Indeed or LinkedIn jobs?

No. Those sites don’t offer public feeds HireWire can read. A browser extension that scores postings on them is built but not yet published.

Can I find remote jobs from Germany?

Yes. Several feeds are remote job boards; set “remote” in your profile and use blocked locations to hide roles restricted to countries you can’t work from.
