Job tracker vs spreadsheet

A spreadsheet is the most flexible tracker there is. It just doesn’t do anything on its own. Here is when a local app earns its place, and when it doesn’t.

HireWire · last reviewed 2026-10-14

Short answer

  • Use a spreadsheet if most of your roles come from referrals, recruiters or sites a tracker can’t read, or if you want custom columns and formulas.
  • Use a local app like HireWire if you want roles to come to you, ranked, with stage dates, follow-up flags and pay filtering handled for you, still with no account.
  • Use both if you do both. Many active searches do.

Where spreadsheets win

Total flexibility. You can add a column for visa sponsorship, commute time or the name of the friend who referred you. You can share a link with a career coach. Nothing breaks if you apply through a channel no tool supports. And a spreadsheet will still open in ten years.

Where a local app wins

The parts of a search that are tedious by hand. Checking the same boards every morning, working out whether a €75,000 role clears a $90k floor, remembering which application went quiet two weeks ago, and counting how many applications turned into screens. HireWire does these automatically, and it does it without an account, so there’s no cloud trade-off.

A combined set-up that works

  1. Use HireWire as your discovery and pipeline for roles on the wire. Pin, stage and follow up there.
  2. Keep a spreadsheet for everything else: referrals, recruiter emails, local roles. The recruiter posts guide gives a column layout.
  3. Once a week, export HireWire’s pipeline as CSV (open the console with ` or Ctrl+K and type export csv pipeline) if you want one combined view. Columns: title, company, source, remote, location, posted, salary, score, status, note, url.
  4. Back up HireWire (Backup everything) whenever you update the spreadsheet.

FAQ

Is a spreadsheet good enough to track job applications?

Yes, for many people. It is flexible and durable. A tracker app adds automatic stage dates, follow-up reminders, metrics and, in HireWire’s case, a ranked feed of new roles.

Can I export HireWire to Google Sheets or Excel?

Yes. Open the console (press ` or Ctrl+K) and type export csv pipeline to download your pipeline as CSV. The CSV button in the sidebar exports the current scored shortlist. Then open or import the file in your spreadsheet.

Can I import a spreadsheet into HireWire?

No. HireWire has no spreadsheet import, and it can only save postings that appear on its feeds.
