2. What HireWire is

Free: a job feed that pulls public postings from job boards and employer career pages, an application tracker, the ghost-job and salary checkers, exports and backups, and a small number of free AI fit scores. These run in your browser and store your data in your browser’s local storage.

Paid (optional): passes that unlock more AI features for a fixed time. See section 7.

HireWire is a tool. We are not a recruiter, employment agency or career adviser, we don’t forward your applications, and we don’t guarantee interviews or a job.

6. AI features: what they are and aren’t

  • AI fit scores, suggested fixes, cover letters, interview prep, negotiation scripts and tailored CVs are produced by a third-party AI model (currently Google Gemini; with your own key, Anthropic’s Claude).
  • The output can be wrong, incomplete, outdated or not right for you. A score is an estimate, not a prediction of what an employer or applicant-tracking system will do.
  • It is not career, legal, tax, immigration or financial advice. For decisions that matter (salary negotiation, contracts, visas) talk to a qualified person.
  • You decide what you send. Read every draft, remove anything that isn’t true for you, and fill in any placeholders. HireWire flags numbers and quotes it can’t find in your CV, but that check isn’t perfect.
  • The ghost-job risk and salary checks are estimates based on public postings. Job postings come from third parties; we don’t check that they are real, current or lawful.
  • You keep all rights to your CV and other content. You allow us (and our AI provider) to process it only to give you the result you asked for. As far as we are concerned, the output is yours to use.

9. How buying a pass works

  1. In the app or extension, choose a pass. You are sent to Stripe’s checkout page.
  2. On that page you see the pass, its duration and the total price. You enter your email and payment details, and you can correct any entry with the normal editing functions of your browser until you pay. Leaving or closing the page cancels the purchase.
  3. You tick the box to accept these terms and to ask us to start your pass immediately (see section 11), then press the pay button. That button sends a binding order. The contract is concluded when the payment succeeds.
  4. You return to hirewire.fyi, which activates your pass and shows your pass ID.

The contract language is English. We don’t store a copy of the contract text for you; these terms are always available at hirewire.fyi/terms.html, and you can save or print them.
