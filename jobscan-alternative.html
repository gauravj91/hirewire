A Jobscan alternative without the subscription

Jobscan is built around résumé scanning for applicant-tracking systems. HireWire comes at it from the other side: find the right postings first, then score your CV against the ones worth applying to, with no account and no recurring bill.

HireWire · last reviewed 2026-10-15

Short answer

  • Choose Jobscan if you want an ATS-style résumé scan with match rates, unlimited scans and AI optimisation, LinkedIn optimisation and auto-apply credits, and you’re fine with a subscription and an account.
  • Choose HireWire if you want a fit score with concrete fixes for the few jobs you really apply to, a free job feed and tracker, and your search kept in your browser. It does not simulate an ATS or check CV formatting.

Disclosure: HireWire publishes this page. Jobscan facts come from Jobscan’s pricing and job-tracker pages, checked on 15 Oct 2026.

Is a one-time pass better than a subscription?

It depends on how you search. Scanning a résumé against dozens of postings a week favours unlimited scans. If you apply to a handful of carefully chosen roles, paying once for 7 or 30 days, with no renewal to remember to cancel, is simpler. HireWire’s pass is per browser and needs no account: you get a pass ID to restore it on another device.

What HireWire doesn’t do

HireWire doesn’t parse your CV file, check its layout or predict how a particular applicant-tracking system will read it. Its score is an AI judgement of fit and wording, and its rewrites only re-phrase what your CV already says (numbers it can’t find become placeholders for you to fill). If your worry is formatting, see our ATS tips for CVs (in German) or use a dedicated scanner.

What you get for free

  • A ranked feed of postings from 55 public sources, refreshed hourly.
  • A private pipeline with notes, contacts, follow-up reminders and email drafts.
  • A free keyword gap check of any pasted posting against your profile skills (Tailor tab).
  • A ghost-job risk and a salary reality check.

FAQ

Is there a free Jobscan alternative?

For tracking and finding jobs, HireWire is free with no account. Its AI fit score gives 3 free scores, then costs a one-time pass (€9 for 7 days, €19 for 30 days) or is free with your own Anthropic API key.

How much does Jobscan cost?

Per Jobscan’s pricing page on 15 Oct 2026: $49.95 billed monthly, or $89.95 every 3 months after a 7-day free trial. The free plan includes 5 résumé scans a month.

Does HireWire check if my CV is ATS-friendly?

No. It rates how well your CV fits a specific posting and suggests better wording. It doesn’t analyse your file’s formatting.

Is my CV stored when I use the AI fit score?

No. It is sent only when you press Score, used for that one request and not stored or logged by HireWire. The full data flow is on the privacy page.
